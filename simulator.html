Try it yourself

The heat-to-heal simulator.

A crack opens in the asphalt and the capsules have released their oil. Set the road temperature, press Play, and watch the healing. Or pick a place in Kenya and run a few days.

Three things to try

  1. Compare 25°C and 45°C. Warm roads heal much faster.
  2. Try 65°C. It heals a little more slowly than 55°C in our model, because very hot oil may drain away. That is our assumption.
  3. Open "A day in Kenya" and compare Mombasa with Eldoret. Mombasa spends about 8 hours a day above 40°C, Eldoret about 3.6.

Read this first. This is an illustrative model. The shape (faster when warm, then levelling off) follows published findings. The numbers are our own design estimates, not published values and not measurements. Real lab results range from a few percent to about 80%. Evidence T2
