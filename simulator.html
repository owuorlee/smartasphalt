Try it yourself

The heat-to-heal simulator.

A crack has opened and the capsules have released their oil. Set the road temperature and press Play, or pick a place in Kenya and run a few days.

Three things to try

  1. Compare 25°C and 45°C. Warm roads heal much faster.
  2. Try 65°C. In our model it is a little slower than 55°C, because very hot oil may drain away (our assumption).
  3. Open “A day in Kenya” and compare Mombasa with Eldoret.

Read this first. This is an illustrative model. The shape (faster when warm, then levelling off) follows published findings; the numbers are our own design estimates, not measurements. Real lab results range from a few percent to about 80%. Source T2
