# Smart Asphalt: source ledger

Every important claim in the research, checked against public sources on 2-3 October 2026. Statuses: **Verified** (confirmed against sources), **Partly verified**, **Our model (untested)** and **Not verified**.

Summary: 20 verified, 5 partly verified, 7 our model (untested), 1 not verified (total 33).

## The waste

### W1. Plastic waste produced in Kenya  [Verified; confidence: High]
- **Claim:** Kenya generates 0.5-1.3 million tonnes of plastic waste a year, of which about 8% is recycled.
- **What the sources say:** Estimates are higher: 0.5-1.3 million tonnes a year, of which about 8% is recycled (Kenya Plastics Pact). UNEP's SMEP programme reports more than 966,000 tonnes. Other sources give recycling at 2-8% (NEMA), 7% (USAID) or 15% (an NEMA official, 2020).
- Source: [IPS News, 'Kenya's hits and misses...' quoting the Kenya Plastics Pact](https://www.ipsnews.net/2023/06/kenyas-hits-and-misses-on-journey-to-eliminating-plastic-waste/)
- Source: [Nation Africa, 'Ridding rivers of plastics' (UNEP SMEP figure)](https://nation.africa/kenya/healthy-nation/ridding-rivers-of-plastics-3479932)

### W2. Waste tyres  [Verified; confidence: Medium]
- **Claim:** Kenya generates more than 3 million waste tyres a year.
- **What the sources say:** More than 3 million waste tyres a year, growing about 10% a year. In 2024 about 3.2 million were generated and roughly 0.4 million (11%) recycled.
- Source: [Centre for Science and Environment presentation (EcoPro, Kenya, 2024)](https://cdn.cseindia.org/attachments/0.62154400_1758776794_anthony-maina.pdf)

### W3. Used cooking oil  [Verified; confidence: Medium]
- **Claim:** Up to about 280,000 tonnes of used cooking oil could arise in Kenya each year.
- **What the sources say:** Kenya consumes about 700,000 tonnes of vegetable oil and fat a year and about 40% ends up as waste, which implies up to about 280,000 tonnes. Only a fraction is collected. Eni has exported used cooking oil collected in Nairobi for biorefining, and GilOil produces biodiesel from it.
- **Note:** Only a fraction is collected. Competing demand (export, biodiesel) means not all oil is available for roads.
- Source: [Business Daily data hub, 'Biodiesel offers Kenya a viable clean transport route'](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/data-hub/biodiesel-offers-kenya-viable-clean-transport-route-3733790)
- Source: [Eni press release, first export of used cooking oil from Kenya (2022)](https://eni.com/content/dam/enicom/documents/press-release/migrated/2022-en/10/PR-eni-starts-exporting-vegetable-oil-for-biorefining-from-kenya.pdf)

### W4. Sugarcane bagasse  [Verified; confidence: Medium]
- **Claim:** Kenya produces about 2.4 million tonnes of bagasse a year; about 1 million tonnes is dumped or burned.
- **What the sources say:** About 2.4 million tonnes of bagasse a year is produced. Much of it is burned to power the mills (for example the 35 MW Mumias and 40 MW West Kenya co-generation plants). Roughly one million tonnes a year is dumped or burned, about 40%.
- **Note:** Bagasse already has a competing use (power), which matters for a clean-energy audience.
- Source: [Norges Vel, 'Waste being used to create energy and jobs in Kenya'](https://www.norgesvel.com/projects/waste-being-used-to-create-energy-and-jobs-in-kenya)
- Source: [UNFCCC CDM: 35 MW bagasse co-generation project, Mumias Sugar](https://cdm.unfccc.int/Projects/Validation/DB/8TC5R6O85VGTZHE2W51KHKWBPIELQ4/view.html)

### W5. Seaweed for alginate  [Partly verified; confidence: Medium]
- **Claim:** Kenya is a net importer of alginate, and red seaweed is farmed in Kwale.
- **What the sources say:** Kenya is a net importer of agar and alginate, and Eucheuma and Kappaphycus are farmed in Kwale. Supply of brown seaweed (Sargassum, the usual alginate source) in Kenya is not yet established.
- **Note:** Our plan is to import alginate first, which avoids depending on local brown seaweed.
- Source: [Seaweeds of potential economic importance in Kenya: field survey and future prospects](https://marineagronomy.org/node/1220)
- Source: [ACTS, Seaweed farming for women in Kwale County](https://blueeconomy.acts-net.org/blog/seaweed-farming-an-economic-breakthrough-for-women-in-kwale-county-kenya)


## The technology

### T1. Calcium alginate capsules survive asphalt production  [Verified; confidence: High]
- **Claim:** Calcium-alginate capsules containing a rejuvenator survive asphalt production.
- **What the sources say:** Confirmed in published TU Delft work: calcium alginate capsules holding a rejuvenator survived asphalt production and compaction, and showed a healing effect on asphalt mastic. Capsules in porous asphalt kept a perfect round shape under X-ray tomography.
- Source: [Xu, Liu, Tabakovic, Schlangen (2019), Materials, calcium alginate capsules in porous asphalt](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6337454)
- Source: [TU Delft: calcium alginate capsules encapsulating rejuvenator as healing system for asphalt mastic (2018)](https://pure.tudelft.nl/portal/en/publications/calcium-alginate-capsules-encapsulating-rejuvenator-as-healing-system-for-asphalt-mastic(bc7e5310-d4c4-4077-9178-f5837169175b).html)

### T2. How much do capsules heal cracks?  [Verified; confidence: High (that results vary widely)]
- **Claim:** Calcium-alginate capsules can improve the healing of cracks in asphalt; results depend on temperature and capsule design.
- **What the sources say:** Published results vary widely. One porous-asphalt study at 23 C found capsules raised healing by about 6 percentage points (maximum 19%); other studies report up to about 80% healing, and a capsule mix recovering 52.9% of strength at 20 C against 14.0% without capsules. Heating above 40 C is reported to have a significant effect on healing.
- **Note:** The animation and simulator use an illustrative model. Its numbers are our own estimates, not published values.
- Source: [Xu et al. (2019), Materials: +6 points at 23 C](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6337454)
- Source: [Comprehensive self-healing evaluation of asphalt concrete containing encapsulated rejuvenator](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9145983)
- Source: [Asphalt self-healing with microvasculars: effects of content, time and temperature](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10342629)

### T3. Do capsules break only at cracks?  [Partly verified; confidence: Medium]
- **Claim:** Capsules are designed to stay shut in everyday traffic and to open at crack tips.
- **What the sources say:** Partly confirmed: capsule rupture under load is documented. One study reported more than 80% capsule rupture after 350 cycles at 0.6 MPa, which means capsules can also break under repeated heavy load, not only at cracks. Our statement that they ignore everyday traffic is a design intent, not a proven result.
- Source: [Self-healing capacity of asphalt concrete with polymer capsules under cyclic loads](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12654166/)

### T4. Plastic-modified roads are used elsewhere  [Partly verified; confidence: Medium]
- **Claim:** Waste plastic is already used in roads in several countries.
- **What the sources say:** India has built about 2,900 km of national highways and about 40,000 km of rural roads using waste plastic (as of 2024). Ghana is cited among countries using plastic in roads. South Africa was only 'embarking' on plastic roads in 2019, so it is not a major example.
- Source: [IDEAS, Plastic Roads India (2025)](https://ideassonline.org/public/pdf/PlasticRoadsIndia2025-ENG.pdf)
- Source: [UK Government K4D helpdesk report 595, Use of waste plastics in road construction](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5d41b34040f0b60a86a5e5dc/595_Use_of_Waste_Plastics_in_Road_Construction.pdf)
- Source: [Mail & Guardian (2019), Plastic is paving an eco-way](https://mg.co.za/business/2019-09-20-plastic-is-paving-an-eco-way/)

### T5. Plastic improves rutting and fatigue resistance  [Verified; confidence: Medium]
- **Claim:** Recycled plastic at about 6% of the binder improves rutting and fatigue resistance.
- **What the sources say:** Supported: reviews report that recycled PE and PP improve rutting and fatigue resistance, and that the best results are usually at 3-8% plastic in the wet method. Our 6% sits in that range. Exact percentage gains in Phase 4 are our own predictions.
- Source: [Frontiers in Materials (2022), waste plastic in asphalt](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/materials/articles/10.3389/fmats.2022.814551/pdf)

### T6. Polyethylene separates from bitumen unless a compatibiliser is used  [Verified; confidence: Medium]
- **Claim:** A compatibiliser additive stops polyethylene separating from bitumen.
- **What the sources say:** Supported: PE-modified bitumen shows severe phase separation because of poor compatibility, and maleic-anhydride grafting is a documented way to improve compatibility.
- Source: [Structurally well-defined functionalized polyolefins as bitumen modifiers (Gdansk University of Technology)](https://pub.pg.edu.pl/en/publication/160377/structurally-well-defined-functionalized-polyolefins-and-graft-copolymers-thereof-as-bitumen-modifie)

### T7. Do plastic roads release microplastics?  [Partly verified; confidence: Medium]
- **Claim:** Whether plastic-modified roads release microplastics.
- **What the sources say:** Not yet settled. Studies estimate microplastic release from plastic-modified pavement at about three orders of magnitude below tyre-wear particles, but it is still uncertain whether particles are released under normal wear. This is an open research question.
- Source: [Penn State: Investigating the environmental impact of post-consumer plastic in asphalt](https://iee.psu.edu/news/investigating-environmental-impact-post-consumer-plastic-asphalt)
- Source: [TRID: Potential release of microplastics from recycled-plastic-modified asphalt](https://trid.trb.org/View/2682917)

### T8. Waste cooking oil as a bitumen rejuvenator  [Verified; confidence: Medium]
- **Claim:** Waste cooking oil can rejuvenate aged bitumen.
- **What the sources say:** Supported by laboratory studies: waste cooking oil softens and rejuvenates aged bitumen and improves low-temperature behaviour and fatigue life, at some cost to rutting resistance. Commercial bio-based rejuvenators exist (for example Cargill Anova and Sylvaroad). A 2026 study found 5% dosages kept good fatigue performance after ageing, while 10-15% caused excessive softening.
- Source: [University of Malaya: waste cooking oil as bitumen rejuvenator](https://eprints.um.edu.my/12758/)
- Source: [Cargill Anova asphalt solutions](https://www.cargill.com/bioindustrial/anova-asphalt-solutions)
- Source: [Materials and Structures (2026): ageing of bio-based rejuvenators in 30% RAP binder](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1617/s11527-026-03195-1)

### T9. Warm Mix Asphalt saves fuel  [Verified; confidence: Medium]
- **Claim:** Warm Mix Asphalt cuts plant fuel use by about 20%.
- **What the sources say:** Measured savings are around 20-22%: an average of 22.1% across six plants (West et al.) and about 20% in a multi-state study.
- Source: [Lee (University of Iowa): WMA usage expanded](https://davidlee.lab.uiowa.edu/sites/davidlee.lab.uiowa.edu/files/2023-01/lee_-_wma_usage_expanded.pdf)
