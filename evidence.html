Evidence

Every claim, its source, and how sure we are.

44 claims behind Smart Asphalt, from Dandora and Kenya’s roads to the environmental questions, each checked against public sources. Facts are labelled verified or partly verified; our own predictions are labelled as models.

How we checked

For each claim we looked for a primary or reputable source: official statistics, peer-reviewed papers, university repositories, organisations’ own reports and reputable news. Import figures come directly from the UN Comtrade database. Where no source could be found, the claim is labelled as our model or not verified. Some sources were read through summaries; these are marked medium confidence.
