The evidence

Every claim, its source, and how sure we are.

We checked 33 claims from our research against public sources on 2 and 3 October 2026. Each claim shows what the sources say, our confidence, and a link to the source.

20 verified 5 partly verified7 our models1 not verified
How we checked

For each headline claim we searched for a primary or reputable source (official statistics, peer-reviewed papers, university repositories, organisations' own pages, reputable news). Import figures were read directly from the UN Comtrade public API. When we could not find a source, we labelled the claim as not verified or as our own model, and did not present it as fact.

Limits of this check. It was done in about a day. Some sources were read in full, others only through search summaries (these are marked medium confidence). Peer-reviewed numbers were not independently reproduced. Detailed engineering figures in the reports (for example specific rheology or mix-design values) were not individually checked: they are background from the technical literature or our own design, and the reports say so.
