What we have not proven

The honest limits.

A good idea is not the same as a proven one. Here is exactly where Smart Asphalt stands.

StageLaboratory-validation concept (TRL 4–5). Each component is documented in published research; our combination has not yet been made or tested, and there is no road trial yet.
Healing amountUncertain: published results run from a few percentage points to about 80%. Our simulator numbers are illustrative. Source T2
Capsules and trafficCapsules should open at cracks, but one study saw many break under repeated heavy load. To be tested. Source T3
EnvironmentFumes, leaching and micro/nanoplastic release are not fully settled for any plastic road. We will measure them. Source E1Source E2Source E3
Cost and lifetimeBuild cost, life-cycle savings and design life are our model, not measurements. Source M2Source M3
JobsThe pilot creates six jobs. We will publish real job and income numbers from the pilot rather than promise large figures now.
Waste supplySome waste streams have other buyers (cooking oil for biodiesel, bagasse for mill power), so we focus on plastic film. Source W3Source W4
Open data gapsPer-kilometre material totals across our reports are not yet reconciled. Source M8

What would change our mind

  • Lab tests showing capsules add little healing at Kenyan temperatures, or do not survive mixing.
  • Capsules breaking under normal heavy traffic rather than at cracks.
  • Unacceptable fumes, leaching or particle release.
  • Measured costs that do not beat conventional asphalt over its life.
