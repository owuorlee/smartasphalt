Glossary

Terms in plain words.

alginate
A jelly-like material from brown seaweed. It forms the shell of our repair capsules.
asphalt
The black road surface: crushed stone held together by bitumen.
binder
The glue part of asphalt. Normally bitumen; in Smart Asphalt, bitumen plus recycled plastic and repair capsules.
bitumen
The black, sticky petroleum product that glues a road's stones together. Kenya imports all of it.
capsule
A microscopic bead, thinner than a hair, with a seaweed-based shell and a plant-oil core.
compatibiliser
An additive that stops plastic and bitumen separating, like an emulsifier in salad dressing.
design target
A number we aim for, predicted from published research and our own model. Not measured yet.
healing index
How much of a crack's lost strength comes back after healing, as a percentage.
hot mix
Asphalt made by mixing stone and binder at about 150-170 C at an asphalt plant.
LDPE / PP
Two common plastics: LDPE is soft film such as bags and wrappers; PP is stiffer, such as bottle caps and tubs.
microplastics
Plastic particles smaller than 5 mm.
nano-silica
An extremely fine glass-like powder that makes the capsule shell stronger and more heat-resistant.
nanoplastics
Plastic particles smaller than about one micrometre, too small to see even with an ordinary microscope.
pavement
The layered structure of a road; the top layer is the asphalt surface.
RAP
Reclaimed Asphalt Pavement: old road material that is milled up and reused.
rejuvenator
An oil that softens and refreshes old, hardened bitumen, like moisturiser for the road.
TAM / SAM / SOM
Total, serviceable and obtainable market: everything possible, what we can reach, and what we aim to win.
TRL
Technology Readiness Level, a 1-9 scale. TRL 4-5 means proven in a laboratory, not yet on a real road.
warm-mix
Asphalt made 20-40 C cooler using additives. It saves plant fuel and is gentler on the capsules.
