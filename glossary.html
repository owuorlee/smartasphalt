Glossary

Terms in plain words.

alginate
A jelly-like substance extracted from brown seaweed. Used here as the capsule shell.
asphalt
The road surface material: crushed stone held together by bitumen.
binder
The glue part of asphalt. Usually bitumen, here bitumen plus plastic and capsules.
bitumen
The black, sticky petroleum product that holds a road's stones together. It is the glue of the road.
compatibiliser
An additive that stops plastic and bitumen from separating, like an emulsifier in salad dressing.
design target
A number we aim for, predicted from research and our model. Not measured.
healing index
How much of a crack's lost strength comes back after healing, as a percentage.
ionic gelation
A way of making jelly beads: dripping alginate into a calcium solution makes it set instantly.
microplastics
Tiny plastic particles. Whether plastic-modified roads release them is still being studied.
nano-silica
Extremely fine glass-like powder that makes the capsule shell stronger.
pavement
The surface layer of a road.
RAP
Reclaimed Asphalt Pavement: old road material that is milled up and reused.
rejuvenator
An oil that softens and refreshes old, hardened bitumen.
Superpave
A road-material specification system that grades bitumen by the temperatures it must survive.
TRL
Technology Readiness Level, a 1 to 9 scale. TRL 4-5 means proven in a laboratory, not yet on a real road.
warm-mix
Asphalt made at lower temperatures with additives, which saves plant fuel.
