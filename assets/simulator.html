The crack starts open and filled with plant oil. The shape of the curves (healing is faster when the road is warm, then levels off) follows published findings that heating above 40°C speeds healing. The numbers are our own illustrative design estimates, not published values: real lab results range from a few percent to about 80%, depending on the capsule and the test. Daily temperatures are illustrative cycles inside our estimated climate-zone ranges. Below 20°C healing is assumed to slow further. Not a measurement.
