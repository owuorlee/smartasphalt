The research

Four chapters of research, explained simply.

Smart Asphalt rests on a four-phase study. Each chapter below says what was asked, what was found, and how sure we are.

1

Can a road heal itself?

The question
Can capsules of plant oil, made from materials available in Kenya, repair cracks in asphalt?
What we found
Yes in principle: calcium-alginate capsules have survived asphalt production and healed cracks in published lab work. We designed a version with seaweed-alginate shells and a cooking-oil and castor-oil core.
How sure we are
Sure that capsules can survive production Source T1. Not sure how much our recipe will heal Source T2.

Read the report

2

What does Kenya's bitumen dependence cost?

The question
How much does Kenya spend on imported bitumen, and which solutions look most promising?
What we found
Kenya imported USD 27.5 M of bitumen in 2023 and USD 20.1 M in 2024, mostly from Gulf-region suppliers. Recycling old asphalt and plastic-modified binders are the quickest wins; self-healing capsules are a strong next step.
How sure we are
Sure about the import data Source P1Source P2Source P3. The scoring of options and long-term savings are our judgement.

Read the report

3

What are the alternatives to pure bitumen?

The question
What is the full range of road binders, and which fit Kenya?
What we found
Ten technologies, from polymer-modified bitumen to bitumen-free concrete. The best path for Kenya is to combine local options: plastic, cooking oil, recycled asphalt and warm-mix.
How sure we are
Each technology exists in research or use Source T4Source T8Source T9. Kenya-specific savings are our estimates.

Read the report

4

Can we put it all together?

The question
Can a plastic-modified, self-healing, lower-bitumen binder be designed for Kenya’s climates?
What we found
Yes on paper: Smart Asphalt, with three climate-zone variants, a manufacturing workflow, a risk analysis and a pilot roadmap.
How sure we are
All performance and cost figures are design targets Source M1Source M2Source M3. The full report contains exact formulations and is available on request.
