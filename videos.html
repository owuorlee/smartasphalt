How a crack heals (20 seconds, with sound)

The animation follows our design: a crack grows, reaches a capsule, the shell breaks, plant oil flows into the crack, and the sun warms the road so the crack closes. The healing is sped up and the numbers on the gauge come from our illustrative model, not from measurements. Turn the sound on: it was composed to match each step.
